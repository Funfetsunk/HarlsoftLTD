About Harlsoft Ltd

Founded in 2011, Harlsoft Ltd has grown from a boutique technology advisory firm into a full-service software consultancy trusted by organisations across the UK and Europe. We believe the best technology outcomes emerge from deep partnerships — where we think alongside our clients, not just for them.

Built on expertise, driven by outcomes

From our founding in Leeds to offices in London and Edinburgh, Harlsoft has always put client outcomes at the centre of everything we do. We bring together technologists, strategists, and delivery specialists to help businesses navigate complex transformation programmes — on time, on budget, and with lasting impact.

Over the past decade we have delivered more than 400 engagements spanning cloud migration, custom platform development, data strategy, and digital product design. Our clients range from FTSE 250 enterprises to rapidly growing scale-ups, but our approach remains consistent: understand deeply, design carefully, deliver reliably.

We are proud to be a Certified B Corporation, reflecting our commitment to responsible business practices, an inclusive workplace, and measurable positive impact beyond profit.

SEC // 03 — Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about working with Harlsoft Ltd.

What does Harlsoft Ltd specialise in? −

Harlsoft Ltd specialises in end-to-end software consultancy for mid-market and enterprise organisations. Our core capability areas are cloud migration and infrastructure modernisation, custom platform and product development, data strategy and analytics, and digital transformation programme management. We work across the full delivery lifecycle — from discovery and architecture through to build, test, and post-launch support.

Which industries do you work with? +

We have deep sector experience across financial services, insurance, retail and e-commerce, media and publishing, logistics, and the public sector. While our consultants bring sector-specific knowledge, we find that the technology and delivery challenges our clients face transcend industry boundaries — and we actively share learnings across verticals to bring fresh perspectives to every engagement.

How does Harlsoft approach new client engagements? +

Every engagement begins with a structured discovery phase, typically two to four weeks, during which we meet key stakeholders, review existing systems and documentation, and develop a clear picture of your objectives, constraints, and risks. From discovery we produce a delivery roadmap and a recommended engagement model — whether that is a dedicated team, a time-and-materials arrangement, or a fixed-scope project. We will never recommend a model that does not genuinely suit your needs.

Do you offer fixed-price projects? +

Yes, for well-defined scopes we are happy to work on a fixed-price basis. We use our discovery phase to ensure we understand the requirements thoroughly before committing to a price. For more exploratory or iterative programmes, we typically recommend a time-and-materials model with clear sprint cadences and regular budget reviews, which gives you both flexibility and predictability. Our commercial team will work with you to find the arrangement that best fits your procurement requirements.

Where are your offices located? +

Our headquarters are in Leeds, with additional offices in London (Shoreditch) and Edinburgh. We operate on a hybrid model — our consultants work on-site with clients when collaboration demands it and remotely when deep focus work is required. We are comfortable working with clients anywhere in the UK and have delivered remote-first engagements for clients in Europe and North America.

How do I get started with Harlsoft? +

The easiest way to start a conversation is through our contact form. Tell us a little about your organisation and the challenge you are trying to solve, and one of our client partners will be in touch within one working day. If you have a more urgent requirement, you are welcome to call our main office number listed on the contact page. We do not use gatekeeping sales processes — you will speak to a senior consultant from your very first conversation.
